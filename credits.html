Works Cited & Traditional Frameworks

The Canadian Encyclopedia

Entry: “Indigenous Peoples’ Medicine in Canada”

URL: https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/native-medicines [cite: 239]

Provided baseline data on cross-hemispheric medicinal timelines, holistic health methodologies, and cultural validation metrics[cite: 2, 7].

Manitou Mounds Nature Guide

Entry: “Medicinal Plant Guide | Manitou Mounds”

URL: https://manitoumounds.com/nature/medicinal-plant-guide/ [cite: 240]

Source for the systematically indexed Anishinaabemowin botanical naming conventions and native prairie ecology data profiles[cite: 119, 120, 125, 131].

Folk Medicine Historical Review

Author: Sieling, Peter

Chapter: “Chapter Four: Native American Medicine” (Jan. 2003, p. 17)

Database: EBSCOhost Collection [cite: 241]

Supplied historical epidemiology comparative framework metrics, systemic symmetry records, and Doctrine of Signatures analyses[cite: 24, 28, 39, 48].

Otipemisiwak Métis Government

Entry: “Traditional Plants” Educational Series

URL: https://albertametis.com/app/uploads/2021/09/Youth-Department-Traditional-Plant-Flashcards.pdf [cite: 242]

Provided specialized interior habitat descriptions, Muskego designations, and traditional harvest preparation records[cite: 53, 68].
