Sources and Classroom Details

This section displays the school project details and shows exactly where all of our research data came from.

School Project Disclaimer

PLEASE NOTE: This website is an educational project made for a High School English Independent Study Unit [ISU]. The information is meant for classroom review, history tracking, and language studies only. It is not professional medical advice, harvesting instructions, or health guidelines. Never pick wild plants or use them for health issues without speaking to a doctor or a certified community elder first.

Works Cited and References

The Canadian Encyclopedia

Entry: “Indigenous Peoples’ Medicine in Canada”

URL: https://thecanadianencyclopedia.ca/en/article/native-medicines

Used for general historical facts, community timelines, and baseline details on holistic healing concepts.

Manitou Mounds Nature Guide

Entry: “Medicinal Plant Guide | Manitou Mounds”

URL: https://manitoumounds.com/nature/medicinal-plant-guide/

The primary source used for our indexed Anishinaabemowin language plant names and native prairie growth details.

Folk Medicine Historical Review

Author: Sieling, Peter

Chapter: “Chapter Four: Native American Medicine” [Jan. 2003, p. 17]

Database: EBSCOhost Collection

Provided facts about early historical health levels, old patterns of disease sharing, and the Doctrine of Signatures details.

Otipemisiwak Métis Government

Entry: “Traditional Plants” Educational Series

URL: https://albertametis.com/app/uploads/2021/09/Youth-Department-Traditional-Plant-Flashcards.pdf

Used to check specific native habitat layouts, regional plant descriptions, and traditional uses.
