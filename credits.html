Works Cited & Reference Database

An alphabetized record of historical texts, clinical drug registries, and Indigenous plant frameworks compiled to bridge ethnobotanical history with modern medical pharmacology.

⚠️ Academic Disclaimer & Safety Notice

This website is an educational project created for an academic assignment to analyze the relationships between ethnobotany and modern pharmacy. The information provided here is for historical, cultural, and educational research purposes only. It is not intended to be used as medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Many of the wild plants described contain high concentrations of highly dangerous or fatal toxins and should never be harvested, consumed, or applied without professional medical guidance and traditional knowledge. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.
